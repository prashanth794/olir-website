Privacy enquiries ↗

What you share with us

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. When you send an enquiry, we collect the name, email address and message you enter in the contact form.

How we use your details

We use waitlist details for Olir launch news and occasional brand updates. We use contact form details to respond to your enquiry, including requests to update or remove your information.

Our website services

Netlify hosts this website and processes form submissions for Olir. Submissions may also be delivered to our email inbox. These services may process information outside Australia under their own privacy terms.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser makes requests to Google to display those fonts. Hosting and font providers may process technical details such as your IP address when you visit.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or remove your details, or stop receiving Olir updates, through our contact form. Please use the email address associated with your request so we can identify the relevant details.

Questions

If you have a question about how your information is handled, contact Olir.

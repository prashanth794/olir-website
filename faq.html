When can I buy Olir?+

Our first hair oil is in development. Join the waitlist for the launch date, size, price and release details as they are confirmed.

What is in the hair oil?+

Rosemary, tea tree oil, aloe vera essence and olive oil are the four key ingredients in our current sample. We will share the complete ingredient list before launch.

Is joining the waitlist a pre-order?+

No. Joining is free and does not reserve a bottle, place an order or take a payment. It simply keeps you in the loop.

Where will Olir ship?+

Olir is being prepared for the Australian market. Delivery areas, costs and timeframes will be published before orders open.

How should I use Olir?+

The final directions, recommended amount and product precautions will be available with the first release. Our ritual page shares the thinking behind the product, rather than final application instructions.

Is the full formula suitable for me?+

We cannot yet confirm suitability for particular skin or scalp conditions. Please wait for the full ingredient list and precautions before deciding whether Olir is right for you.

How do I contact Olir or leave the waitlist?+

Use our contact form for product questions, launch enquiries or a request to remove your details from the waitlist.
